Equation 1 Elementary Micro-Strata Index (Jevons Geometric Mean)
$$I_{J, r, t} = \left( \prod_{k=1}^{n_{r,t}} \frac{p_{r,t,k}}{p_{r,0}} \right)^{\frac{1}{n_{r,t}}} \times 100 = \exp\left( \frac{1}{n_{r,t}} \sum_{k=1}^{n_{r,t}} \ln \frac{p_{r,t,k}}{p_{r,0}} \right) \times 100$$
Why Jevons: Eliminates the upward substitution bias of Carli (arithmetic mean). Assumes unitary price elasticity of demand within homogeneous route micro-strata.
Equation 2 Upper-Level National Rollup (Laspeyres Traffic Weighting)
$$APIx_t = \sum_{r=1}^{M} w_r \cdot I_{J, r, t} \quad \text{where} \quad \sum_{r=1}^M w_r = 1.000$$

Base Fare

Governing Rule: Rule 135 of The Aircraft Rules, 1937. Tariff is formally deregulated; airlines establish revenue via dynamic yield management inventory buckets (RBDs).

Derivation Formula: Base Fare = Pre-Tax Airfare × (Base Split Ratio ≈ 68%)
Volatile Energy Surcharge (~25%–35%)

Fuel Surcharge (ATF)

Governing Rule: Ministry of Civil Aviation Circular (2008). Permitted unbundling of jet fuel costs to absorb OMC (IOCL, BPCL, HPCL) benchmark fluctuations without altering base fare tables.

Derivation Formula: Fuel Surcharge = Pre-Tax Airfare × (Fuel Split Ratio ≈ 32%)
Airport & Security Levy (₹250–₹710)

UDF / PSF (Airport Charges)

Governing Rule: Section 13 of AERA Act, 2008 & BCAS Aviation Security Order. Fixed per-passenger charges for airport modernization, runway CAPEX, and CISF security.

Statutory Rates: DEL: ₹550 | BOM: ₹490 | BLR: ₹450 | HYD: ₹420 | Non-Metro: ₹250–₹350
Statutory Tax (5% Eco / 12% Biz)

Goods and Services Tax (GST)

Governing Rule: CBIC Notification No. 11/2017-Central Tax (Rate), HSN Code 9964. Economy passenger air transport attracts 5% GST (without input tax credit on goods). Business class attracts 12% GST with full ITC.

Derivation Formula: GST = (Base Fare + Fuel Surcharge) × 5% [Economy] or 12% [Business]
OTA Checkout Surcharge (₹0–₹350)

Convenience Fee

Governing Rule: DGCA CAR Series M Part IV & Consumer Protection E-Commerce Rules. Non-refundable payment gateway handling and booking service fee applied per ticket segment.

Portal Standard Fees: MakeMyTrip: ₹350 | Cleartrip: ₹299 | Ixigo: ₹299 | Google Flights/Direct: ₹0
UDF User Development Fee

Levied by airport operators (AAI, GMR, Adani) under AERA approval to fund terminal modernization, runway expansion, and airport infrastructure.

PSF Passenger Service Fee

Statutory facilitation charge collected by airlines and remitted to the National Aviation Security Fee trust for CISF security personnel at airports.

ATF Aviation Turbine Fuel

Specialized kerosene-based jet fuel representing ~35%–45% of total airline operating expenditure, updated bi-monthly by state oil marketing refiners.

GST Goods and Services Tax

Unified indirect consumption tax in India administered by CBIC; passenger air travel is classified under Services Accounting Code (SAC) 9964.

AERA Airports Economic Regulatory Authority of India

Statutory tariff regulator established under the AERA Act, 2008 to determine aeronautical tariffs and User Development Fees across major Indian airports.

DGCA Directorate General of Civil Aviation

India’s national civil aviation regulatory body overseeing aviation safety, airworthiness, licensing, and airfare tariff transparency under Rule 135.

MoCA Ministry of Civil Aviation

Union government ministry responsible for civil aviation policy formulation, regulatory frameworks, regional connectivity (UDAN), and bilateral air treaties.

OTA Online Travel Agency

Digital flight booking portals (e.g. MakeMyTrip, Cleartrip, Ixigo) providing flight search aggregation and charging checkout convenience fees.

OMC Oil Marketing Company

Public sector oil corporations (Indian Oil, Bharat Petroleum, Hindustan Petroleum) that supply jet fuel and establish domestic benchmark ATF prices.
